Supply Chain Reading

I am looking at supply-chain questions from OM and economics: how firms source, hold inventory, recover from disruptions and adapt their networks. AI and OR papers belong in the main discussion when they address those supply-chain decisions or applications. General methods are kept separately as background.

General methods and frameworks — optional background, 5

These are not the supply-chain core or evidence of a supply-chain trend by themselves. ORLM studies general automated optimization modeling; Dai–Swaminathan surveys AI across operations. SPO, Bertsimas–Kallus and Ban–Rudin can inform an inventory or sourcing study, but their general methods should be distinguished from a supply-chain-specific research question. Their original priority labels below are historical, not a new recommendation to prioritize them.
